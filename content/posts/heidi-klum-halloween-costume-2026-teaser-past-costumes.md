> **The short version:** The 2026 Heidi Klum Halloween costume is still under wraps, but a one-week-out Instagram teaser has fans guessing. She has promised a look that is "very, very bloody," the reveal comes at her annual party on October 31, and the top theories range from The Substance to The Last of Us. Here is everything she has said, plus every costume since 2000.

The Heidi Klum Halloween costume reveal is one of pop culture's most anticipated annual moments, and the 2026 guessing game is officially on. One week before her party, the supermodel and TV host posted a strange close-up that has fans zooming in for clues. Below is what she has actually revealed, the most popular theories, and a full year-by-year look back at the costumes that earned her the title "Queen of Halloween."

## What Heidi Klum revealed about her 2026 Halloween costume

On **October 24**, Klum shared the first look on Instagram. According to [E! News](https://www.eonline.com/news/1437448/heidi-klum-teases-grotesque-halloween-costume-ahead-of-2026-party), her caption read: "What's my costume? One week to go…. #HeidiHalloween," followed by a string of spooky emojis.

The image itself gives little away. [Parade](https://parade.com/celebrities/heidi-klums-teases-halloween-costume) describes it as a coral-like close-up of colorful prosthetic pieces, arranged in a texture that could pass for meat or organs, possibly a brain or intestines.

### The clues she has dropped

Klum has been teasing this one for weeks:

- In a September interview with People, she said the costume will be **"very, very bloody,"** E! News reports.
- She added, "That's what Halloween is all about. We go gory, and we go there."
- To Parade in September she said: **"I will give you one clue. It's bloody."**
- She also told Parade, "I loved when I made myself really old. That's a lot of fun," a nod to her 2013 elderly costume.

Put together, the clues point to something gory and heavy on prosthetics, which fits her recent run of full-body transformations.

## Fan theories: what is Heidi Klum dressing as?

Fans flooded the comments with guesses. The most popular, according to E! News:

| Theory | Why fans think so |
| --- | --- |
| **Monstro Elisasue** from *The Substance* (2024) | The fleshy, mutated texture and the "bloody" clue |
| **Clickers** from *The Last of Us* | The fungal, coral-like growths in the close-up |
| **Something underwater**, like the Great Barrier Reef | The image looks like coral |

Parade frames the debate as whether Klum is "going underwater, or somewhere far gorier." Given her promise of blood, the horror-movie guesses are leading, but she has fooled fans before. Nobody predicted a worm in 2022.

## When and where is the party?

Klum's annual Halloween party takes place on **October 31**, and the costume is traditionally revealed on the red carpet that night, according to E! News. Parade notes the bash has been a New York tradition since 2000, which makes 2026 another milestone year for one of the longest-running celebrity Halloween events.

Expect photos and video to appear on her Instagram and across entertainment outlets that evening, U.S. time. This year Halloween also falls on the same weekend that [daylight saving time ends](/blog/daylight-saving-time-ends-2026-fall-back-november-1), so party-goers get a bonus hour.

### What her recent costumes tell us

Klum's last few years show a clear pattern: huge, prosthetic-heavy builds that take hours.

- **2025 Medusa:** "I really wanted to have snakes that were moving, a super-ugly, super-scary face, really ugly teeth," she said, according to E! News.
- **2024 E.T.:** "It was so heavy holding that head on my head... I thought my neck was going to snap off," she said, per E! News.
- **2023 peacock:** a full ensemble with nine performers.
- **2022 worm:** a full-body prosthetic suit, followed by a quick change later in the night.

A 2026 costume that is "very, very bloody" with intricate prosthetic textures fits that trend perfectly.

### How to follow the reveal

If you want to see the costume as soon as it drops, follow Klum's official Instagram account, where she posted this year's teaser with the #HeidiHalloween hashtag. Entertainment outlets such as E! News, People and Parade typically publish red carpet galleries within hours of her arrival. Because the party is in New York, the reveal usually lands on the evening of October 31 Eastern time, which is early morning November 1 in Europe and Asia.

## Why the reveal goes viral every year

The Heidi Klum Halloween reveal works for the same reasons a movie trailer does: a long tease, a single big reveal, and a visual that is easy to share. Several things keep it trending:

1. **The countdown.** Klum's teasers start weeks ahead, building search interest daily.
2. **Unpredictability.** Her choices swing from glamorous (Jessica Rabbit) to grotesque (the worm), so guessing is half the fun.
3. **Behind-the-scenes content.** Makeup and prosthetic videos give fans a second wave of clips after the party.
4. **Couple costumes.** Since 2018 her husband Tom Kaulitz has often joined in, from Shrek to Medusa's stone warrior.

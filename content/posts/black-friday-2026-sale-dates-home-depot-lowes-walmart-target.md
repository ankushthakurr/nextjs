Here are the official dates announced so far, what each store is offering, and who gets early access.

## When is Black Friday 2026?

Black Friday is **Friday, November 27, 2026**, and Cyber Monday is **Monday, November 30, 2026**. That much is fixed by the calendar. What is not fixed is when the deals actually begin, because nearly every big retailer now runs a multi-week "Black Friday season" that starts in late October or early November and stretches past Cyber Monday.

That matters for shoppers in two ways. First, many of the advertised prices are available well before the Friday itself, so waiting until Thanksgiving weekend is no longer required. Second, the best-known "doorbuster" style items are increasingly released in timed drops, often with an early window reserved for loyalty or paid members.

## Home Depot Black Friday 2026: dates and deals

The Home Depot announced on October 29 that its **Black Friday Savings Event runs November 5 through 25**, in stores and online, according to the company's [press release](https://corporate.homedepot.com/news/products/home-depot-kicks-black-friday-season). The company notes that product selection, pricing and promotions may vary between stores and the website, and that select online offers are available throughout the ad.

Some categories run on their own schedule. [USA TODAY](https://www.usatoday.com/story/shopping/deals/black-friday/2026/10/29/the-home-depot-black-friday-deals-2026-dates/92491345007/) reports the Tool Gift Center is live from October 26 through January 31, 2027, Black Friday appliance deals run November 4 to December 2, and outdoor power equipment deals run November 5 to December 6.

Deals Home Depot has named so far include:

- **Holiday decor:** 6.5-foot LED pre-lit artificial Christmas trees for $49.98, select 3.5-foot LED pre-lit inflatables for $19.98, and 100-count LED string lights from $5.98.
- **Appliance bundles:** up to an extra $500 off qualifying refrigeration purchases of $2,498 or more, up to $300 off laundry purchases of $1,698 or more, up to $300 off cooking purchases of $1,498 or more, and up to $150 off dishwasher purchases of $798 or more, with a maximum discount of $1,250.
- **Power tool BOGOs:** buy a participating tool or battery kit from brands including Ryobi, Milwaukee and DeWalt and get a select power tool free.

### The free Ryobi tool offer

The headline that sent many people searching is USA TODAY's report that Home Depot is "giving away" free Ryobi tools. In practice it is a buy-one-get-one offer. USA TODAY says the Ryobi One+ 1/2-inch drill and circular saw combo kit is 42% off at under $80, and buyers can add a free tool-only item such as a compact workshop blower, 13-inch string trimmer, oscillating multi-tool, reciprocating saw or the Whisper Series 7-1/2 inch fan. "Tool only" means no battery or charger is included, so the deal works best if you already own Ryobi One+ batteries.

USA TODAY also lists a Milwaukee M18 5Ah two-pack starter kit at 65% off, DeWalt power tools at up to $130 off, outdoor power equipment at up to $250 off, and appliance markdowns such as $1,200 off a GE Profile four-door French-door refrigerator.

"Black Friday is an amazing opportunity for customers to find great value on all they need for the holiday season," said Rodes Bazzel, senior vice president of merchandising at The Home Depot, in the company's announcement.

## Lowe's Black Friday 2026: four weeks and member perks

Lowe's moved first. According to [USA TODAY Network reporting](https://www.timesonline.com/story/shopping/deals/black-friday/2026/10/29/lowes-black-friday-2026-home-improvement-sale/92500849007/), the retailer launched **four weeks of Black Friday deals on Thursday, October 29**, covering tools, appliances, holiday decorations and outdoor power equipment. A separate **12 Days of Deals** promotion begins November 16.

Lowe's did not publish specific sale prices in its announcement. Most of the news is about its free MyLowe's Rewards loyalty program, which gives members:

- Early access to Black Friday Doorbusters one day before they open to all shoppers.
- Free same-day delivery on eligible purchases during the holidays, with a $25 minimum and limits on size, weight, availability and ordering deadlines.
- Early access to new Traeger grills and accessories on Lowes.com in November, plus an offer to earn 3X points on eligible Traeger purchases.
- Free Holiday Kids Workshops, including a Holiday Engine build on November 14 and a Holiday Trolley Car on December 12.

One practical note: **Lowe's stores will be closed on Thanksgiving Day**, though online-only deals stay available.

## Walmart Black Friday 2026: three events and Walmart+ early access

Walmart announced its plan on October 26. In a [company release](https://corporate.walmart.com/news/2026/10/walmart-unwraps-three-major-holiday-shopping-events), it laid out three separate events:

1. **Early Black Friday Deals, November 12 to 15.** Deals go live on Walmart.com at 12:01 a.m. ET and in stores at 6 a.m. local time.
2. **Black Friday Deals, November 23 to 29.** Online deals start at 12:01 a.m. ET, and in-store deals start on Black Friday itself, November 27, at 6 a.m. local time.
3. **Cyber Monday, November 30 to December 2,** online starting at 12:01 a.m. ET.

Walmart+ members get early access to "hot Deal drops" at 12:01 a.m. ET on November 12 and again on November 23. Whatever is left opens to all customers 24 hours later. During both Black Friday events, select online-only deals are also reserved for Walmart+ members for the first 24 hours, while supplies last.

Walmart has not yet published a full list of prices, though its release highlights thousands of gifts under $20 and a top toys list with 25 items priced under $25. "This season, we're bringing customers and members incredible savings across three major shopping events," said Julie Barber, chief merchandising officer for Walmart U.S.

## How to shop Black Friday 2026 without overpaying

The longer season gives shoppers more chances at a good price, but it also makes it easier to buy too early. A few habits help:

- **Write down the price you see today.** With sales running from late October into December, the same item may show up at several prices. A note or screenshot makes it easy to tell whether a "Black Friday" price is actually lower.
- **Check what membership is required.** Walmart's earliest drops are for paid Walmart+ members, while Lowe's perks require the free MyLowe's Rewards program. Decide whether early access is worth it before the drop, not during it.
- **Read the fine print on bundles.** Home Depot's appliance savings depend on spending thresholds, and its free tools are tool-only. Make sure the bundle fits what you would have bought anyway.
- **Expect online and in-store differences.** Home Depot says selection and pricing can vary by channel, and Lowe's stores are closed on Thanksgiving even though its website is open.
- **Mind the clock change.** Daylight saving time ends this weekend, so early-morning online drops will feel an hour different. Our guide to [when clocks fall back](/blog/daylight-saving-time-ends-2026-fall-back-november-1) has the details.

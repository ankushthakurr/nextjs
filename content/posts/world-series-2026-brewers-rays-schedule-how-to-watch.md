## How to watch the World Series on TV and streaming

- **TV:** FOX (English) and FOX Deportes (Spanish)
- **Streaming:** FOX One
- **Start time:** 8 p.m. ET / 5 p.m. PT for every game

Live TV streaming services that carry your local FOX station also show the games. If you use an antenna, your local FOX affiliate broadcasts every game for free.

## Game 1 recap: Brewers hold on 3-2

The opener on October 23 had a little of everything. According to [AP](https://apnews.com/article/world-series-rays-brewers-game-1-049d8efa7d3d7c8fc8a4e4b88d8dfe77):

- **A record first pitch.** NL Cy Young Award favorite Jacob Misiorowski opened the game with a 103.5 mph fastball to Yandy Díaz, the fastest pitch ever thrown in a World Series. "The adrenaline kind of took over, and I tensed up and threw it hard," he said.
- **Starter's line.** Misiorowski allowed one run and four hits over five innings, with a season-low three strikeouts, and earned the win.
- **Bullpen lockdown.** Abner Uribe and Aaron Ashby shut the Rays down through the eighth. [Sportsnet](https://www.sportsnet.ca/mlb/article/brewers-beat-rays-behind-strong-pitching-in-game-1-of-world-series/) notes the late-inning trio of Uribe, Ashby and Trevor Megill combined for four shutout innings.
- **Big bats.** Jackson Chourio and William Contreras each had two hits and an RBI, with a key hit from Christian Yelich, per Sportsnet. Contreras also threw out two runners from behind the plate.

### The chaotic ninth inning

The ending was wild. The Brewers thought they had won on a double play and poured out of the dugout, but a replay review overturned the call at second base, Sportsnet reports. Megill regrouped and struck out Jonny DeLuca on a 2-2 pitch with two runners in scoring position.

"It's good to get the first win out of the way," Megill told AP. "You've got to think worst-case scenario there."

Tampa Bay went 0 for 6 with runners in scoring position, AP notes. And per Yahoo Sports, citing USA Today's Bob Nightengale, it was "the first time since the 2000 World Series that no one homered in Game 1 of a World Series, and just the second time since 1987."

## Game 2: Rays jump out early in Milwaukee

Game 2 was still in progress at the time of writing. Here is how it unfolded through four innings, according to the Milwaukee Journal Sentinel's [live updates](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/brewers-vs-rays-live-updates-210001686.html) on Yahoo Sports:

- **Starters:** Logan Henderson for Milwaukee, Drew Rasmussen for Tampa Bay, as NBC Sports had previewed.
- **Rays' fast start:** Yandy Díaz reached on an error by shortstop Cooper Pratt, then Chandler Simpson and Junior Caminero drove in runs and Liam Hicks hit a two-run homer. Tampa Bay led 4-0 in the first.
- **Brewers respond:** RBI singles from William Contreras and Garrett Mitchell cut it to 4-2.
- **Bullpen game:** JoJo Romero relieved Henderson and threw a quiet fourth. In the bottom of the fourth, a Rays error on a David Hamilton grounder scored Pratt to make it 4-3, and Rasmussen left with the bases loaded. Cam Booser got Christian Yelich to ground out, and Dustin May entered for Milwaukee in the fifth.

## Why this World Series is historic

Both franchises are still waiting for a first title:

| Team | Previous World Series | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Milwaukee Brewers | 1982 | Lost to St. Louis Cardinals in 7 games |
| Tampa Bay Rays | 2008 | Lost to Philadelphia Phillies, 4-1 |
| Tampa Bay Rays | 2020 | Lost to Los Angeles Dodgers, 4-2 |

*Source: NBC Sports.*

AP points out this is the first World Series featuring two teams seeking their first championship since **1980**, when the Philadelphia Phillies beat the Kansas City Royals in six games. Game 1 was also the Brewers' first World Series win since 1982, according to Sportsnet.

### What the Game 1 win means

History favors the opener's winner. According to AP, the Game 1 winner has gone on to win the World Series **62.8% of the time**. That is an edge, not a guarantee, especially in a series this evenly matched.

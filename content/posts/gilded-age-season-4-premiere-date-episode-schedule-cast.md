> **The short version:** The Gilded Age season 4 premieres Sunday, November 1, 2026, at 9 p.m. ET on HBO and HBO Max. The season has eight episodes, airing weekly through a December 20 finale. Bertha and George Russell start the season estranged, Agnes is planning to host President Grover Cleveland, and Jim Gaffigan and Neal Huff join the cast as Cleveland and John D. Rockefeller.

The Gilded Age season 4 is days away, and searches for the show and its cast spiked overnight after HBO held the season's New York premiere on October 26. Julian Fellowes' period drama comes back after its most-watched season yet, with a cliffhanger to resolve, a fractured marriage at the center of the story, and a handful of new historical figures walking into Fifth Avenue drawing rooms. Here is everything confirmed so far: the date and time, the full episode schedule, how to watch, who is new, who is gone, and what the trailer gives away.

## Full episode release schedule

HBO is sticking with a weekly rollout rather than a binge drop. According to Deadline, the eight episodes land on these Sundays, all at 9 p.m. ET:

- **Episode 1:** Sunday, November 1
- **Episode 2:** Sunday, November 8
- **Episode 3:** Sunday, November 15
- **Episode 4:** Sunday, November 22
- **Episode 5:** Sunday, November 29 (Thanksgiving weekend)
- **Episode 6:** Sunday, December 6
- **Episode 7:** Sunday, December 13
- **Episode 8 (finale):** Sunday, December 20

That timing puts the finale right before the holidays, which gives the show a clean eight-week run with no breaks.

## How to watch The Gilded Age season 4

Each episode airs on the HBO cable channel and streams at the same time on HBO Max. Decider lists HBO Max at **$10.99 a month with ads** and **$18.49 a month ad-free**. The Disney+, Hulu and HBO Max bundle costs **$19.99 a month with ads** or **$32.99 a month without**, according to the same report. Decider also notes episodes can be bought on Prime Video if you do not want a subscription.

If you need to catch up, season 3 is the one to rewatch, because almost every storyline in the new season picks up directly from its finale.

## Where season 3 left off

Season 3 ended with the Russell household split down the middle. Per Decider's recap, Bertha (Carrie Coon) married her daughter Gladys (Taissa Farmiga) to an English duke, the Duke of Buckingham (Ben Lamb), against the wishes of George (Morgan Spector), who wanted Gladys to find her own love match. The fallout split Bertha and George, and Gladys closed the season by telling her mother she was pregnant.

Elsewhere in the finale:

- George was shot and nearly died, and his fired associate Richard Clay became a prime suspect, as Deadline recounted.
- Dr. William Kirkland (Jordan Donica) proposed to Peggy Scott (Denée Benton).
- Enid Winterton (Kelley Curran) and Oscar van Rhijn (Blake Ritson) formed an alliance.
- Jack Trotter (Ben Ahlers) left service to work as an inventor.

## What happens in season 4: the trailer and story

HBO's official description, as reported by Deadline, says Bertha's family deals with the consequences of how she has changed society, Agnes van Rhijn (Christine Baranski) sees an opening to climb back to her former standing, Marian (Louisa Jacobson) forges a new path, and Peggy faces an uphill battle to win over her future in-laws. The logline boils it down to "be careful what you wish for."

The [full trailer, released October 13](https://deadline.com/2026/10/the-gilded-age-season-4-trailer-bertha-george-agnes-1237156081/), adds detail:

### Bertha and George

George looks furious from the first frame. "Everything is wrong," he says, and "Rockefeller is trying to drive me to my knees." Bertha begs him to come home, and he snaps back: "So like you to look for a weakness to lure me back." Oscar sums up the gossip around them, saying the Russells' rise to the top "might have left their marriage on shaky ground."

### Agnes, Ada and the President

Agnes is preparing to entertain President Grover Cleveland at home, but the money now belongs to her sister Ada (Cynthia Nixon). Ada warns that it is all becoming "too much" and says she will not pay for anything more, which sets up the kind of sibling sparring the show does best.

### Peggy, Marian and Larry

Peggy and Dr. Kirkland hit trouble when a possible job offer for him threatens to clash with her career. Marian, meanwhile, has a run-in with Larry Russell (Harry Richardson).

Variety also reports that Bertha's grandson arrives in the season premiere, and the show is building a subplot about anti-monopoly legislation, which fits George's war with Rockefeller.

## The Gilded Age season 4 cast: who is new and who is gone

### New faces

The biggest additions are historical. [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/tv/features/the-gilded-age-season-4-cast-creator-popularity-1236910185/) reports that comedian **Jim Gaffigan** plays **President Grover Cleveland** and newcomer **Neal Huff** plays **John D. Rockefeller**. Deadline lists other new names joining this season: Dallas Roberts, Elizabeth Marvel, Andrew Burnap, Taylor Trensch, James Scully, Dennis Haysbert, Maggie Kuntz, Bonnie Milligan and Adrienne Warren. The reports we read did not give their character names.

### Who is back

The core ensemble returns, including Christine Baranski, Cynthia Nixon, Louisa Jacobson, Carrie Coon, Morgan Spector, Taissa Farmiga, Harry Richardson, Denée Benton, Jordan Donica, Audra McDonald, Blake Ritson, Kelley Curran, Ben Ahlers, Ben Lamb, Donna Murphy, Simon Jones and Jack Gilpin, per Deadline. Many of them walked the red carpet at the New York premiere on October 26, along with Fellowes, Warfield, Gaffigan and Haysbert, according to Deadline's photo gallery published on Yahoo.

### Who is not returning

[Deadline reported](https://deadline.com/2026/10/the-gilded-age-characters-not-returning-season-4-1237168161/) that two longtime supporting players will not be back: **Patrick Page** as Richard Clay, the ex-Russell associate suspected in George's shooting, and **Sullivan Jones** as newspaper publisher T. Thomas Fortune, whose storyline ended after a fistfight with Dr. Kirkland. Deadline framed it as a big ensemble taking some characters out of rotation.

## Why season 4 matters for HBO, and what about season 5

The show's rise has been slow and then sudden. Variety reports the season 3 finale drew **5 million viewers in three days**, an 88% jump over that season's premiere, and every episode in the back half of the season set a viewership record. Season 3 also earned **eight Emmy nominations**, more than the first two seasons combined.

That came after HBO trimmed the budget. Season 1 reportedly cost about $100 million, Variety says, and HBO pared things back for season 3, encouraging the writers to lean into character over spectacle. "We never felt the difference," HBO's Francesca Orsi told Variety.

The cast sounds aware of how unusual that turnaround is. "None of us take for granted that getting a fourth season of the show right now is, not exactly a miracle, but you feel like you're part of a very fortunate minority at this point," Morgan Spector said. Cynthia Nixon pointed to the show's themes: "It is kind of staggering how many parallels there are to this moment that we're in."

### Will there be a Gilded Age season 5?

Not officially, based on the reports we read today. After the 2026 Emmys, HBO content chief Casey Bloys told Deadline, as quoted by [Show Snob](https://showsnob.com/the-gilded-age-season-5-gets-optimistic-update-ahead-season-4-premiere), "We are talking to them about what a fifth season would look like, stay tuned on that." Bloys added that Morgan Spector's role in Netflix's *The Secret of Secrets* should not get in the way.

The creative team clearly wants more. "I want this show to go for 10 seasons if it can," Orsi told Variety, and Fellowes said: "We'll go on writing it as long as we're asked."

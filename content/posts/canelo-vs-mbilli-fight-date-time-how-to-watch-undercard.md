> **The short version:** Canelo vs Mbilli is on for Saturday, October 31, at Kingdom Arena in Riyadh, with Canelo Alvarez challenging unbeaten WBC super middleweight champion Christian Mbilli. The card streams on DAZN pay-per-view, the broadcast starts at 9:15 a.m. ET, and the main event comes hours later. Organizers insist the show will go ahead despite a U.S. order pulling non-emergency embassy staff out of Saudi Arabia.

Canelo vs Mbilli is the biggest boxing event of Halloween weekend and the first time Saul "Canelo" Alvarez has fought since losing his undisputed super middleweight crown to Terence Crawford in September 2025. It is also a fight being staged under unusual pressure: Riyadh's main airport has been hit during the regional conflict, travel warnings have been raised, and at least one fighter on the undercard has not booked a flight. Here is what fans need to know, from the start time and price to the stakes and the security picture.

One note on time zones: U.S. clocks do not fall back until the following morning, November 1 (see our guide to [daylight saving time ending](/blog/daylight-saving-time-ends-2026-fall-back-november-1)). So on fight day 9:15 a.m. ET is 6:15 a.m. PT, 1:15 p.m. in the U.K., and 4:15 p.m. in Riyadh. Treat those as the start of the broadcast, not the main event.

## How to watch the Canelo fight

DAZN holds the rights worldwide. In the U.S., Bad Left Hook lists the standalone pay-per-view at $79.99, and the event is also part of DAZN's Ultimate tier for subscribers who already pay for it. ESPN confirms the same arrangement in the U.K.: DAZN pay-per-view, or the Ultimate tier.

Practical tips if you plan to watch live:

- **Buy early and test your login** on the device you will use. Big pay-per-view nights tend to bring last-minute sign-ups.
- **Do not set an alarm for 9:15 a.m. ET expecting the main event.** That is when coverage begins. DAZN has said the main card will start at least a few hours later, and ring-walk times have not been published.
- **Check fight week updates.** Grand arrivals are Wednesday, October 28, the press conference is Thursday, October 29, and the ceremonial weigh-in is Friday, October 30, according to [BoxingScene](https://www.boxingscene.com/articles/canelo-alvarez-christian-mbilli-still-going-ahead-despite-us-state-department-ordering-embassy-staff-to-leave-saudi-arabia). Final running times usually firm up by then.

## Who is Christian Mbilli?

Christian Mbilli is the champion here, which flips the usual script for a Canelo fight. He is 29-0-1 with 24 knockouts, according to Yahoo Sports, and MARCA describes him as a French-Cameroonian pressure fighter with real knockout power.

His path to the belt was quick and a little unusual. According to [talkSPORT](https://talksport.com/boxing/4290724/canelo-alvarez-vs-christian-mbilli-date-time-undercard-venue-channel-live-stream/):

1. He won the WBC interim super middleweight title by stopping Poland's Maciej Sulecki in one round in June 2025.
2. He was held to a draw by Lester Martinez in a Fight of the Year contender on the Canelo vs Crawford undercard in Las Vegas in September 2025. That draw is the only blemish on his record.
3. In January 2026, the WBC elevated him from interim to full champion after it stripped Crawford for unpaid sanctioning fees.

Mbilli has not been shy about the moment. "My last fight was the fight of the year (contender)," he said in comments reported by talkSPORT. "And when the fight is over, the world will witness a historic victory for me."

## Where Canelo stands at 36

Canelo is 36 and 63-3-2 with 39 knockouts, per Yahoo Sports and talkSPORT. MARCA notes this will be his 69th professional fight. His last outing was a unanimous decision loss to Crawford in Las Vegas in September 2025, which cost him all four of his 168-pound belts.

This fight is a direct route back to one of them. Canelo has framed it as a fresh start rather than a farewell. "After so many years in this sport, my motivation is still the same, to challenge myself, represent Mexico, and continue building my legacy," he said, as quoted by talkSPORT. He also offered respect to his opponent: "Mbilli is undefeated and a great fighter, and I respect that."

The bout was first pitched for September 12 in Riyadh, on Mexican Independence Day weekend, and his own early quote referenced that date. It later moved to Halloween.

## The matchup: what the experts expect

ESPN and Yahoo Sports both list Canelo as the favorite. The man who beat him last time agrees. Crawford said Mbilli's aggressive, forward style plays into Canelo's hands: "Mbilli's style is perfect and he won't be able to do anything when Canelo applies pressure; I don't see any chance for him to win," according to [MARCA](https://www.marca.com/en/boxing/2026/10/23/terence-crawford-backs-clear-winner-in-the-canelo-alvarez-vs-christian-mbilli-fight.html).

Yahoo Sports' preview also picks Canelo, predicting he has "enough of his old magic left" to win on points. Mbilli's camp sees it differently. His promoter, Camille Estephan, told [Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/boxing/news/13602282/canelo-alvarezs-super-middleweight-world-title-fight-with-christian-mbilli-still-set-to-go-on-in-riyadh-despite-conflict) he expects Mbilli to "keep his belt and dethrone Canelo, God willing."

## Canelo vs Mbilli undercard

The card carries several title fights. ESPN, Yahoo Sports and talkSPORT list these as the main bouts:

- **Canelo Alvarez vs. Christian Mbilli**, 12 rounds, WBC super middleweight title
- **Jaime Munguia vs. Nikita Zon**, 12 rounds, Munguia's WBA super middleweight title (co-main event)
- **Rafael Espinoza**, 12 rounds, defending his WBO title. Outlets differ on his opponent: ESPN and talkSPORT list Lorenzo Parra, while Yahoo Sports and Bad Left Hook list Ricardo Blandon.
- **Lindolfo Delgado vs. Arthur Biyarslanov**, 12 rounds, vacant IBF super lightweight title (in doubt, see below)
- **David Picasso vs. Carl Jammes Martin**, 12 rounds
- **Reito Tsutsumi vs. Raymundo Rojas Garcia**, 10 rounds, super featherweights

Prelim bouts listed by talkSPORT and Yahoo include Bek Nurmaganbet vs. Marcos Vazquez, Carlos Emiliano Rojo Vazquez vs. Flavius Biea, Hector Aguirre vs. Brayan Leon Salgado, and Sultan Almohammed vs. Erick Villegas.

### The Delgado fight is in jeopardy

According to KO on SI, Lindolfo Delgado, his trainer Robert Garcia and his team were still in the U.S. with no travel booked to Riyadh as of October 25, citing safety concerns and the difficulty of flying into the region. If he withdraws, the card still has three other title fights.

## Why the Canelo fight in Riyadh is still on

The security backdrop is the biggest open question of fight week. Sky Sports reports that Riyadh's King Khalid airport has been bombed during the ongoing Middle East conflict, that the U.K. government advises against non-essential travel to Saudi Arabia, and that the U.S. tells citizens to reconsider travel there. Tennis' Six Kings Slam in Riyadh has already been postponed.

The U.S. then went further. "The U.S. Department of State issued an ordered departure for non-emergency U.S. government employees and their family members in Saudi Arabia," State Department spokesperson Tommy Pigott said, as reported by BoxingScene. He said "Iranian-supported Houthis have increasingly engaged in hostilities against Saudi Arabia, including attacks on civilian airports and other critical infrastructure resulting in civilian casualties and severe travel disruptions."

Saudi organizers have not moved. Turki Alalshikh, chairman of the General Entertainment Authority, said the fight will go ahead "no matter what happens," per BoxingScene. Mbilli's promoter echoed that: "Christian will arrive in Saudi as planned," Estephan told Sky Sports. KO on SI reports there have never been plans to move the card out of Saudi Arabia.

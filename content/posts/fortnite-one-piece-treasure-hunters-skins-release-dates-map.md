> **The short version:** The Fortnite One Piece season, called Treasure Hunters, launched on November 1, 2026 with a new 80-player island, Crossbones Isle, and One Piece powers like Luffy's Gum-Gum Fruit. The Straw Hat skins roll into the Item Shop in waves through November 13, and the mini-season ends with a Chapter 7 live event on Saturday, December 5.

Fortnite One Piece fans waited through a few hours of overnight downtime, and the collaboration is now live. Epic Games' Treasure Hunters mini-season swaps in a smaller island, adds pirate-themed powers and weapons, and brings the Straw Hat crew into the Item Shop over the next two weeks. It also features music stars A$AP Rocky and Lil Uzi Vert plus virtual singers Hatsune Miku and Kasane Teto. Here is everything confirmed so far: the skin release dates, what is on the map, what the Battle Pass includes, and the key dates to put on your calendar.

## When the Fortnite One Piece season started

The season arrived with update v43.00. According to [North Jersey](https://www.northjersey.com/story/tech/gaming/2026/11/01/fortnite-offline-as-treasure-hunters-one-piece-season-drops-on-nov-1/92515160007/), the Fortnite Status account posted that "Downtime for v43.00 begins November 1 at 1 AM ET (5 AM UTC) with matchmaking ending shortly beforehand." The outlet reported servers were expected back around 6 AM Eastern.

Console players could get a head start. Fortnite posted on Saturday that "Pre-downloads for the v43.00 update begin rolling out tonight at 7 PM EDT (11 PM UTC) on Xbox One, Series X|S and PlayStation 5," North Jersey reported.

Epic first teased the collaboration on October 27 with a trailer. [Crunchyroll News](https://www.crunchyroll.com/news/latest/2026/10/27/fortnite-teases-one-piece-collab-in-treasure-hunters-trailer) noted the teaser showed the Going Merry flying in place of the usual Battle Bus, along with Luffy, Zoro, Nami, Usopp, Sanji and Buggy.

This is a mini-season. North Jersey describes it as lasting just over a month, and Epic's own post points to a Chapter 7 end event on Saturday, December 5.

## Every One Piece skin and its release date

Epic's [official announcement](https://www.fortnite.com/news/fortnite-treasure-hunters-the-greatest-adventure-of-all-time) lays out the Item Shop schedule. VICE groups the Straw Hats into three waves. Here is the full lineup, including the non-anime collaborations arriving at the same time:

| Date (2026) | What arrives | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| November 1 | Luffy, Nami, Usopp | Item Shop (season launch) |
| November 1 | Portgas D. Ace, Nico Robin | Battle Pass |
| November 5, 7 PM ET | A$AP Rocky | Item Shop |
| November 7 | Zoro, Sanji, Chopper Sidekick | Item Shop |
| November 12 | Lil Uzi Vert | Item Shop |
| November 13 | Buggy | Item Shop |
| November 14 | Kasane Teto, Cap'n Hatsune Miku | Item Shop |

A few things worth knowing before you spend V-Bucks:

- **Prices are not announced.** [VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/fortnite-treasure-hunters-shop-dates-all-skins/) points out that prices and the dates these cosmetics leave the shop were not included in the published schedule.
- **Chopper is a Sidekick, not an Outfit.** Forbes' Paul Tassi had guessed as much before Epic confirmed it, writing that Chopper was "going to either be a sidekick or back bling, though almost certainly the former."
- **These are anime designs.** Tassi noted the skins are "the anime skins, not the Netflix show versions that attempt to be 3D and feature the actors' likenesses."

Tassi also called the size of the drop unusual: "This is the most skins I have ever seen from a single collaboration, at least released around the same time."

## Crossbones Isle: the new map and One Piece powers

Treasure Hunters replaces the regular island with **Crossbones Isle**, a smaller 80-player map. Epic says players can "climb into a cannon to launch across" the island and visit two locations from One Piece: **Arlong Park** and **Windmill Village**. Forbes, writing from the reveal trailer, also listed Foosha Village, Loguetown and the Baratie among the points of interest shown on the map.

The headline feature is fighting with One Piece powers. Epic highlights two so far:

- **Gum-Gum Fruit:** lets you fight as Luffy with his stretching powers.
- **Three Sword Style:** Zoro's signature technique for slicing through opponents.

There is a non-anime landmark too. A$AP Rocky gets a battleship point of interest named the **$.$. Rocky**, where players can "rain rockets from above with A$AP Rocky's Shock Rocket," according to Epic. North Jersey reports the season also includes an A$AP Rocky boss challenge.

Epic's framing is that "each week, a new captain arrives in Battle Royale with their own crew and POI." The Straw Hats and A$AP Rocky lead off, followed by Lil Uzi Vert, Cap'n Hatsune Miku and Kasane Teto. Epic also teases "what's inside Captain X's mega chest," a hidden treasure on the island.

### Captain's Days and weekly events

New weapons and Sprites do not all drop at once. Epic is spacing them out on what it calls **Captain's Days**:

- **November 1, 9 AM ET:** first Captain's Day, at season launch
- **November 7, 12, 14 and 21:** more new weapons, Sprites and items

Two recurring events run all season. **Mastery Mondays** and **Treasure Hours Sundays** add Captain's weapons to the start of every match, plus boosted drops, Epic says. Competitive players should watch Fortnite's competitive page, where Epic says One Piece cups will be announced.

## What is in the Treasure Hunters Battle Pass

Two fan-favorite One Piece characters are in the Battle Pass rather than the shop. Epic confirms **Portgas D. Ace** and **Nico Robin** are featured, alongside new anime-style looks for three Fortnite originals: **Jonesy**, **Cuddle Team Leader** and **Charlotte**. North Jersey's rundown also lists anime versions of Hope and Oscar.

There is a gifting bonus. Epic says if you gift the Battle Pass to a friend, you both receive a bonus **Dark Kawaii Team Leader** Outfit style.

Because Ace and Robin come through the Battle Pass while Luffy, Zoro and the rest are shop items, players who only want one or two characters can plan purchases around the release dates in the table above.

## Why the Fortnite One Piece collab is a big deal

Fortnite has run anime crossovers before. Forbes lists Dragon Ball Z, Demon Slayer, Chainsaw Man, Hunter x Hunter and Sailor Moon among past partners. Tassi argues this one is different in scale, calling One Piece "an enormous get for Fortnite, arguably its highest-profile anime series ever." He added: "If Fortnite can land One Piece, I'm guessing it can eventually get anything they want through deal-making and big checks."

The timing also lines up with the anime's calendar. Crunchyroll notes that the One Piece TV anime, based on Eiichiro Oda's manga, is set to return to broadcast in Japan in 2027, after the current Elbaph Arc episodes. A month-long Fortnite season keeps the Straw Hats in front of a huge gaming audience during that gap.

For Epic, the mini-season format is a bridge. Treasure Hunters runs only until the December 5 end event, which wraps up Chapter 7. That makes it a short, high-profile run before whatever comes next.
